## Hardware Specification

- **Airframe**: Meteor65 Pro 65mm Polycarbonate Frame
- **Flight Controller**: BETAFPV F4 1S 5A AIO (Integrated Serial ELRS 2.4G)
- **Motors**: 0802 22000KV Brushless Motors (1.0mm shaft)
- **Propellers**: Gemfan 31mm 3-Blade Props
- **ESC Protocol**: DShot300 (Bluejay 0.20 48kHz PWM)
- **Dry Weight**: 18.2g
- **All-Up Weight (AUW)**: 26.5g (with 1S 300mAh LiHV)
